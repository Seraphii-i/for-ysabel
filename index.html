A letter for my beloved

Ysabel

✝

Of all the souls God could have placed in my life, I thank Him with all my heart that He gave me you.

scroll, my love ↓
I. My heart

You have my whole heart.

I could face the whole world without trembling, yet I become helpless trying to explain what you mean to me. You are my sweet girl, my beloved, and one of the greatest gifts God has placed in my life. I love you more deeply than these little words could ever carry.

Whenever you are away, even for a little while, I miss you more than I know how to explain. Everything feels quieter without you. My heart simply wants you near me: to hear your voice, hold you close, kiss your forehead, make you feel safe, and know that my sweet girl is beside me. No triumph, journey, or grand ambition could ever mean as much as coming home to you.

“Offer me an empire, its crown and all its glory, and I would still choose the quiet privilege of waking beside you for the rest of my life.”

II. Before God

I thank Him for you.

You are not merely someone I love. You are someone I thank God for and someone I carry into my prayers. I ask Him to guard you when I cannot, comfort you when I am far away, hold you through every fear and every difficult night, and make me into a husband who loves you with patience, gentleness, loyalty, and faith.

I do not want a love that burns brightly and disappears. I want to choose you in the ordinary moments too: sleepy mornings, stressful days, laughter in the kitchen, tears held against my chest, prayers before sleep, forgiveness after mistakes, and every season God permits us to walk through together.

III. When I miss you

Distance only teaches my heart your name again.

There are moments when I miss you while I am still talking to you, simply because hearing your voice makes me wish I could reach through the distance and hold you. I want to pull you close when you are tired, brush the worries from your face, kiss your forehead, and remind you that you never have to carry everything alone.

And when the night grows quiet, I find myself thinking of the day there will be no goodbye at the end of a call. I will only have to turn toward you, whisper that I love you, and fall asleep knowing you are home.

IV. Our future

I cannot wait to call you my wife.

I dream not only of our wedding day, but of the thousands of days afterward. I want sleepy mornings beside you, prayers whispered together at night, your hand finding mine beneath the blankets, dinners at our table, Christmas mornings with our children, difficult days when we hold each other a little tighter, and quiet evenings when we are old and still reaching for one another.

♔

Marriage

To stand before God, take your hands in mine, and promise myself to you for life.

⌂

Home

A little kingdom of our own, made warm simply because you are there.

✝

Family

To hear our children laughing through the house and raise them with faith, tenderness, courage, and love.

V. My promise

I want to love you gently, for a lifetime.

I cannot promise you a life without hardship. I can promise that I want to meet it beside you. I want to listen when your heart is heavy, protect the trust you place in me, tell you the truth even when it is difficult, forgive and ask forgiveness, pray with you, laugh with you, and never let familiarity make me careless with your heart.

I want to spend my life learning how to love you better. Not merely loudly, but patiently. Not only when life is beautiful, but especially when it is not.

My dearest Ysabel,

There are moments when missing you feels like exile. Yet I want no distant kingdom and no grander throne. I want the small kingdom of our own home, your head against my chest, your laughter in the next room, and the privilege of caring for your heart every day.

I want your hand trembling in mine at the altar as we make our promises before God. I want to call you my wife. I want sleepy mornings, ridiculous laughter, forehead kisses, prayers in the dark, difficult days we survive together, children we adore, Christmases as a family, and countless ordinary evenings that become precious simply because they are ours.

If God grants me those years with you, I will spend them thanking Him for you. I would count myself richer than any emperor simply to grow old beside you.

You are my beloved, my sweet girl, my answered prayer, and the woman I cannot wait to marry. Wherever life carries us, I want my arms to be a place where you feel loved, cherished, protected, and at home.

Yours, always.

“And now these three remain: faith, hope and love. But the greatest of these is love.”
— 1 Corinthians 13:13

♥

I love you, Ysabel.

Until there is no distance left between us. Until I can finally call you my wife. Until we are old and still reaching for each other’s hands.

I don’t need an empire, my love.
I don’t need glory.

Give me a little home.
Give me our children laughing in the next room.
Give me your hand in mine.
Give me God above us.

And give me a lifetime to love you.

That would be enough.
That would be everything.

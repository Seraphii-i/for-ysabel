to Ysabel

I'm sorry.

I do not know how to say this without feeling like every sentence is too small.

I am sorry for every moment where loving me has been harder than it should have been. I am sorry for every time fear got between what I felt for you and what you actually received from me.

I am sorry for every time I wanted to love you gently and somehow let anxiety turn that gentleness into tension.

I wish I could reach backward into every moment where I made you feel hurt, confused, pressured, doubted, or exhausted and replace it with the version of me that simply held your hand and said, "I trust you. I love you. I'm scared, but I trust you."

the part I hate admitting

I can let fear speak louder than love.

I can overthink tone, timing, silence, distance, small changes, little details.

I can take something innocent and stare at it until my mind turns it into a threat.

I can ask for reassurance, receive it, and still feel afraid five minutes later.

I can become quiet when what I really want is comfort. I can become suspicious when what I really feel is terrified. I can seem distant while every part of me is screaming not to lose you.

Sometimes the ugliest parts of my fear are just love with nowhere safe to go.

That does not excuse them. It just means I need to learn how to carry that fear without placing it in your hands.

what the past left behind

I learned to expect betrayal before I learned how to feel safe.

I have been cheated on. I have been used. I have been manipulated. I have trusted people who made that trust look stupid.

Somewhere along the way, my mind learned to look for the knife before it looked for the hand holding mine.

And sometimes I hate myself for that.

Because you are not them.

You should never have to stand in the shadow of people who hurt me before you.

You should never have to prove that you are innocent of wounds you never gave me.
how I see myself

Sometimes I genuinely struggle to understand why you would choose me.

I see every flaw. Every insecurity. Every ugly thought. Every stupid reaction. Every moment where I know I could have handled something better.

I know how difficult I can be.

I know how badly I overthink. I know how much reassurance I sometimes need. I know how afraid I am of being replaced.

There are moments where I look at myself and think, "Why would anyone choose this when they could choose somebody easier?"

There are moments where I feel like I am too damaged, too complicated, too anxious, too insecure, too much work.

And then you love me anyway.

And I do not know if you understand how much that means to me.

what scares me most

I am terrified that one day my fear could become the thing that hurts you.

That thought genuinely scares me more than I know how to explain.

I never want you to look at me and feel like you have to be careful around me.

I never want you to feel like you have to prove your love every day just so I can breathe.

I never want you to feel guilty for having your own life, your own space, your own friends, your own thoughts, your own bad days.

I never want my love to become a cage built out of my fear.

I want to protect your heart, not make it defend itself from me.
what I am sorry for

I am sorry in ways I do not know how to make sound big enough.

I am sorry for every time my fear sounded like an accusation.

I am sorry for every time I overthought something innocent until it became painful.

I am sorry for every time I needed reassurance again because the first reassurance could not get past the noise in my head.

I am sorry for every time I shut down instead of simply telling you, "I'm scared. Please be gentle with me right now."

I am sorry for every moment where I was difficult to reach, difficult to reassure, difficult to understand, difficult to love.

I am sorry for every moment where my past walked into our present and I let it speak for me.

You should never have to bleed for wounds somebody else gave me.
if I could make you feel one thing

I wish you could feel how much I love you for just one minute.

Not hear me say it. Not read it. Not be reassured by it.

I mean actually feel it exactly the way I do.

I wish I could take everything that happens inside me when I think about you and place it inside your heart for sixty seconds.

I wish you could feel the warmth. The tenderness. The ache. The gratitude. The longing. The fear. The strange peace of imagining you beside me.

I wish you could understand why your name can calm me down and terrify me at the same time, because somebody meaning this much to me means there is suddenly something precious enough to lose.

I do not know how to explain the amount of love I have for you without language eventually failing me.
what you mean to me

You are not just my girlfriend.

When I think about you, I do not just think about messages on a screen or calls late at night.

I think about finally seeing you in person.

I think about hearing your voice beside me instead of through a phone.

I think about holding you for the first time and probably not wanting to let go.

I think about ordinary mornings. Grocery shopping. Cooking together. Annoying each other. Laughing at stupid things. Quiet evenings. Christmas lights. Praying together. Falling asleep beside each other.

I think about marriage. I think about family. I think about growing older. I think about all the completely unremarkable days that become precious simply because they are ours.

You exist in the future I imagine.

That is how much you mean to me.

the fear I cannot describe properly

I am terrified of losing you.

I wish I knew how to explain that without it sounding possessive or unhealthy.

I do not mean that you belong to me. You do not.

I mean that loving you has made you important enough that the possibility of losing what we have scares me deeply.

The thought of waking up one day and realizing that the person I imagined all those mornings with is no longer there is something I struggle to even sit with.

I fear losing your voice. Your affection. Your ridiculous little habits. Your attention. Your trust. Your love. The future I have imagined with you.

And sometimes that fear gets so loud that it becomes stupid.

Sometimes I am so afraid of losing you tomorrow that I forget to properly cherish you today.

I do not want my fear of losing you to steal even one moment of the love I still have the privilege of giving you now.
what I want for you

I want you to feel safe in my love.

I want you to know that you can tell me when I am wrong.

I want you to know that you are allowed to need space.

I want you to know that your feelings matter even when they are inconvenient for me.

I want you to know that loving me should never require shrinking yourself.

I want you to feel cherished, not monitored.

Protected, not controlled.

Wanted, not responsible for keeping me whole.

Loved, not obligated.

I want my love to feel like somewhere you can rest.
as long as I am here

I want to cherish you for the rest of my life.

I want to remember the little things.

I want to learn the expressions on your face before you even say what you are feeling.

I want to know what makes you laugh when you are exhausted. What makes you feel safe when the world is too loud. What meals comfort you. What songs make you quiet. What little traditions become ours.

I want to be there for the beautiful days and the ugly ones.

I want to love you when life feels romantic and when it feels completely ordinary.

I want to choose you when choosing somebody is not exciting but deliberate.

I want to grow old enough with you that we can look back at who we are now and barely recognize ourselves.

And because faith means something to me, part of my heart hopes that love is not simply erased by death; that the good we become through loving each other belongs somehow to eternity too.

if I ever forget

I need to remember what love actually asks of me.

Love does not ask me to eliminate every fear before I am allowed to love you.

It asks me not to make you responsible for those fears.

Love does not mean I will never hurt you by accident.

It means I care enough to listen when I do, apologize without defending myself, and change what needs changing.

Love does not mean holding tighter every time I am afraid.

Sometimes it means opening my hand and trusting the person who is already choosing to hold it.

Ysabel

I love you. I am sorry. I want to love you better.

If I could tell you only one thing after everything on this page, it would be this:

Please never mistake my fear for the size of my love.

My love for you is not the jealousy. It is not the anxiety. It is not the overthinking. It is not the terrified questions my mind sometimes creates.

My love is the part of me that wants you happy even when I am struggling.

It is the part of me that wants to become gentler because you deserve gentleness.

It is the part of me that wants to protect your trust because I know how precious trust is once it has been broken before.

It is the part of me that looks at you and thinks, "God, please let me love this person well."

I never want to hurt you intentionally.

And when I hurt you unintentionally, I never want pride to stop me from seeing it.

I want to cherish you.

I want to cherish the things about you I already know and all the things I have not discovered yet.

I want to cherish the girl I love now and the woman you will become.

I want you beside me through every season God allows us to share.

I want to love you when we are young and stupid, when we are tired and busy, when life is beautiful, when life is cruel, when everything feels easy, and when love becomes something we choose deliberately through difficulty.

I do not want fear to be the loudest thing in my love for you. I want love itself to be louder.

I am sorry for every time I failed at that.

I am sorry for every moment where my wounds made you feel something you never deserved to feel.

I am sorry if I ever made you question whether loving me was safe.

I am sorry if I ever made you feel like my fear mattered more than your peace.

I am so fucking sorry for every moment where all the love inside me somehow came out wrong.

Because, Ysabel, there is so much of it.

More than I know how to say.

More than this website can hold.

More than a message can communicate.

I love you.

I love you when I am calm.

I love you when I am scared.

I love you when my mind is kind to me and when it is tearing me apart.

I love you in the ordinary moments and in every impossible future I keep dreaming about.

And I want to spend my life making sure the way I love you becomes worthy of the words "I love you."

I cannot promise perfection.

I can promise that hurting you is never what I want.

I can promise that your heart matters to me.

I can promise that I want to learn.

I can promise that I want to become safer, gentler, wiser, and better at loving you.

I can promise that I will never consider your love ordinary.

I am sorry.

I love you.

And if I have the privilege of growing old beside you, I hope those two sentences become less and less necessary together, because I learned how to live the second one properly.

for Ysabel — with all the words I keep failing to say properly.

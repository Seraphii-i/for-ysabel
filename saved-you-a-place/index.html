A SMALL SURPRISE ABOUT A VERY LARGE FEELING

You haven't arrived yet.
And somehow, you're already part of home.

For now, you're still on the other side of an ocean.

But here, I've already started imagining the little spaces that will become yours.

01 · THE PASSENGER SEAT

I saved you the seat beside me.

For late-night drives. For terrible singing. For conversations that accidentally last until two in the morning.

I've imagined looking over and seeing you there more times than I'd admit.

02 · THE KITCHEN

I saved you a cup.

Because someday making you something warm won't require wishing you were here to drink it.

Two mugs. One kitchen. No time difference.

03 · BY THE DOOR

I saved you somewhere to come home to.

A jacket by mine. Shoes beside mine. Little evidence everywhere that this isn't only my home anymore.

I want your things to become part of the ordinary scenery of my life.

04 · GOODNIGHT

I saved you the other side.

Not only for the romantic nights. For tired evenings. For difficult days. For the nights one of us is sick, quiet, or completely exhausted.

I just want to be able to reach over and know you're there.

05 · FAITH

I saved you a place beside me.

Somewhere we can pray together. Thank God together. And ask Him to teach us how to love each other properly.

Not perfectly. Faithfully.

06 · CHRISTMAS MORNING

I saved you a Christmas.

No time zones. No screen. No saying goodnight while it's still afternoon for the other person.

Just snow outside, something warm inside, and you actually here.

07 · EVERYTHING AFTER

I saved you a place in my future.

In ordinary Tuesdays. In photographs that haven't been taken. In family dinners that haven't happened. In stories we'll tell our children, God willing.

The life doesn't exist yet. But I want to build it with you.
